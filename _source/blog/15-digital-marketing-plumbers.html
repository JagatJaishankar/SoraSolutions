Getting Found on Google — SEO & GBP for Plumbers

There are two places you want to appear on Google: the Local Pack (the map section with three business listings) and the organic results below it. Both matter, and they work differently.

Google Business Profile — Your #1 Free Asset

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is what gets you into the Local Pack — the three listings that appear at the top of search results for local queries like "plumber near me" or "plumber [suburb]". It's free to set up and, when done properly, it's often the single highest-ROI thing a plumber can do for their marketing. Read our full guide to Google Business Profile for tradies to get the most out of it.

Key things to get right: accurate business hours (including 24-hour emergency hours if you offer them), a complete service area covering every suburb you work in, photos of your team and recent jobs, and a consistent flow of Google review requests after every completed job. Plumbers with 4.8+ stars and 30+ reviews consistently dominate their local pack.

Plumber SEO — Ranking in Organic Results

Plumber SEO is about making your website rank in the organic (non-ad) results for keywords like "emergency plumber [suburb]", "blocked drain [city]", and "hot water system replacement [area]". See our complete SEO guide for tradies for the full breakdown. For suburb-level ranking tactics, our local SEO tips for tradies covers the specifics in detail.

For plumbers, the most valuable pages to build are suburb-specific service pages — one page per service-area combination. "Blocked drain plumber Parramatta", "Hot water replacement Blacktown" — these hyper-local pages have lower competition than broad city terms and convert at higher rates because the searcher is looking for someone close to them right now.

Google Ads for Plumbers

Google Ads is the fastest way to get phone calls for a plumbing business. Unlike SEO, which takes months to build momentum, Google Ads can have your phone ringing within 48 hours of launching a campaign. The trade-off is cost — plumbing keywords are competitive, with CPCs typically ranging from $22 to $35 for high-intent terms in Australian capital cities.

For plumbing specifically, Search campaigns are the workhorse. You're targeting people who are already searching for a plumber — it doesn't get higher intent than that. Local Service Ads (LSAs) are also excellent: you only pay per lead (not per click), and they appear above regular ads in the results. Check our Google Ads guide for tradies for full campaign setup instructions.

Campaign Structure for Plumbers

Structure your campaigns around intent and urgency. Emergency keywords ("emergency plumber", "burst pipe repair", "plumber open now 24 hour") go in their own campaign with the highest bids — these leads are the most urgent and highest value. General service keywords ("blocked drain", "hot water replacement", "gas fitting") get their own campaigns with slightly lower bids and more targeted ad copy.

Negative keywords matter as much as your target keywords. Add "DIY", "how to", "plumbing course", "plumbing supplies", "apprenticeship" as negatives immediately. These searches cost you money and will never convert. A well-maintained negative keyword list can cut wasted ad spend by 25–35%.

💡

Budget guide: For a single-location plumbing business, start with $50–80/day on Google Ads. This gives you enough data within 2–3 weeks to understand what's working. Always set up conversion tracking before spending a dollar — without it, you have no idea which keywords are actually generating phone calls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should a plumber spend on digital marketing?

Most plumbing businesses see strong results spending $1,500–$3,000/month across Google Ads, SEO management, and a decent website. Google Ads alone typically runs $800–$2,000/month depending on your location and competition. The key is starting with your website and Google Business Profile first — those deliver the best long-term return and one is completely free. Never spend on ads until your website is conversion-ready.

What's the best marketing channel for plumbers?

Google is the best channel for plumbers because the search intent is so high — someone searching "emergency plumber Sydney" is ready to book right now. Start with Google Business Profile (free), then build your website and SEO, then add Google Ads for fast lead volume. Facebook ads work better for non-urgent services like bathroom renovations or hot water upgrades. Never lead with Facebook for emergency plumbing — the intent simply isn't there.

How long does plumber SEO take to work?

Realistically, 3–6 months to see meaningful ranking improvements for competitive keywords. Your Google Business Profile can show results faster — often within 4–8 weeks of proper optimisation. That's why we recommend running GBP improvements and Google Ads simultaneously while SEO builds in the background. Read our full SEO guide for tradies for a month-by-month breakdown of what to expect.

Do I need a website if I'm already on Google Business Profile?

Yes — absolutely. Your Google Business Profile gets you into the Local Pack, but your website is where leads go to decide whether to call you or scroll to the next result. Without a website, you're losing every job that goes to plumbers who have one. Think of GBP as getting you in the room and your website as closing the deal. You need both working together.
